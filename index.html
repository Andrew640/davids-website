Biography
David is known for his calm and methodical teaching approach. Alongside private tuition for piano, music theory and organ based in Sanderstead, South Croydon, he is an Accredited Teacher with the Royal College of Organists. David is also the Director of Music at St. John's Church in Shirley, Croydon where he directs the four-part SATB choir of 18 for the weekly choir practice and Sunday Choral Eucharist. David is the Musical Director of the Cantilena Singers, based in Sanderstead. He also regularly accompanies choirs on piano and organ, including the Oxted and Limpsfield Choral Society where recent performances have included Handel's 'Messiah', Rutter's 'Requiem', Karl Jenkins 'The Armed Man' and Puccini's 'Messa de Gloria'.

David graduated from the Royal Academy of Music with an MA in Organ Performance and was also awarded the Stephen Bicknell Organ Prize. Whilst at the RAM, he studied organ with David Titterington and Anne Marsden Thomas. In 2016, he became the first recipient of the Dame Gillian Weir Organ Scholarship at the Royal Festival Hall. He has also held the Organ Scholarship at St. Martin-in-the-Fields in Trafalgar Square. David became a Fellow of the Royal College of Organists in May 2021. He also holds the Licentiate of the Royal Academy of Music in Organ Teaching, the Licentiate of Trinity College London in Organ Performance and the Diploma of the Associated Board of the Royal Schools of Music in Piano Performance. Outside of music, David enjoys swimming. He is also a private pilot.
